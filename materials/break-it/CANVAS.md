# Canvas text for Break It, Fix It

## Canvas page (paste into a page named "Break It, Fix It")

**[Open Break It, Fix It](https://hfgolino.github.io/BuildYourOwnInstrument/break-it/)**

Last week you built a 12-item scale. Today you break it on purpose, six times, and fix it again. Before you see the data, you predict which numbers will change.

**Before you start**
- Use a laptop with an up-to-date browser.
- Have your **Construct Lab exit ticket** (PDF or .txt) on your laptop. If you do not have it, download it from your Construct Lab submission in Canvas.
- Sign in with your full name and UVA computing ID, then click **Choose my Construct Lab ticket** to load last week's scale. Without the ticket, choose a standard scale; the lab works the same way.

**The four stages** (about 30 minutes)
1. **Baseline (4 min):** read the evidence board, explore your scale's EGA network, and answer two questions. Press **i** on any number for its definition.
2. **Round 1, break reliability (9 min):** pad it, make it a mood, cut it to True / False.
3. **Round 2, break validity (9 min):** mix in another trait, use it for hiring, drop a facet. For each break: predict, run it, then fix it.
4. **Exit ticket (4 min):** write the reflection, click **Create my exit ticket**, then **Save as PDF** (Safari: the PDF menu at the bottom left of the print window).

**Submit:** upload the PDF to **Break It, Fix It: Exit Ticket**. If the PDF does not work, upload the .txt file instead. There are no points: the ticket is marked complete or incomplete, and your feedback appears as a comment.
